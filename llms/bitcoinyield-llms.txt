# BitcoinYield

> BitcoinYield ranks where you earn the most on Bitcoin, USDC and stablecoins right now, across DeFi and CeFi, by real APY and risk together via a transparent A-D grade.

BitcoinYield is a live crypto-yield comparison site. It compares reported APY, risk and available liquidity for
tracked opportunities on Bitcoin, SOL, USDC, stablecoins and ETH across DeFi and CeFi (lending, pools,
vaults, staking, fixed-term and custodial earn), graded A-D on a published risk rubric and ranked by real APY and risk together,
never by who pays a commission. For each asset it shows the best proven rate at each risk grade (A lowest risk), so a
holder sees what more risk pays. Source coverage and collection times vary; cite the linked page for the current number.

## API and MCP (for developers and AI agents)
Live crypto yields as JSON, updated every 3 hours. Every endpoint and tool is free and needs no key or account:
- GET /api/v1/yields: List the best yield per asset.
- GET /api/v1/offers: List the top live offers for one asset.
- GET /api/v1/prices: Get every live yield offer (full feed).
- MCP server: /api/mcp (Streamable HTTP, no install). Tools: list_yields (list the best yield per asset), list_offers (list the top live offers for one asset).
  Add {"mcpServers":{"bitcoinyield":{"url":"https://bitcoinyield.co/api/mcp"}}} to any MCP client.
- Machine-readable: /openapi.json (OpenAPI 3.1) and /.well-known/mcp.json.
- Docs, with a working example for every endpoint: /docs.
